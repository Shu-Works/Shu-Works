# 05 — Book Concept

## Book #1: *The Enchanted Witch's Cottage*

> **A Cozy Coloring Journey Through a Witch's Magical Home**
> 50 hand-designed pages · Adult coloring · Printable · 8.5 × 11

---

## The Premise

The buyer is handed a key to a cozy witch's cottage and invited to wander through
it, room by room, season by season — coloring it to life. The book opens at the
garden gate at dusk and ends in the cottage's most magical, intricate spaces. It
is designed to be *traveled*, not flipped through randomly.

## The Feeling We're Selling

Warm candlelight. A cat asleep on an open spellbook. Steam off a teacup. Rain on
the window while herbs dry overhead. **Calm, escape, and the quiet pride of a
finished page.** We sell the evening, not the PDF.

---

## The Journey Structure (four chapters, progressive difficulty)

The 50 pages rise in detail and ambition like a story, so the colorist *grows*
through the book and earns the showpiece finale.

### Chapter 1 — Introduction (Pages 1–12) · *Easy → Light*
**"Arriving at the Cottage."** Single, friendly subjects with generous open
spaces: the gate, the front door, a window box, a teacup, a single pumpkin, a
sleeping cat. Welcoming and confidence-building. Big shapes, low intimidation.

### Chapter 2 — Exploration (Pages 13–26) · *Light → Medium*
**"Through the Rooms."** Small vignettes and corners of the cottage: a shelf of
potions, a reading nook, a windowsill garden, the hearth, a familiar curled among
objects. More elements, still breathable composition.

### Chapter 3 — Discovery (Pages 27–40) · *Medium → Detailed*
**"The Secrets of the Cottage."** Full scenes with depth and layering: the
apothecary wall, the spiral library, the greenhouse, the cluttered-cozy kitchen,
the autumn garden. Richer detail, satisfying complexity.

### Chapter 4 — Masterpiece Collection (Pages 41–50) · *Most Impressive*
**"The Heart of the Cottage."** The ten showpiece pages — the most intricate,
layered, awe-inducing scenes in the book: the grand library at night, the witch's
study in full, the enchanted greenhouse in bloom, the cottage cross-section, the
midnight garden under the moon. These are the pages buyers screenshot and pin.

## Composition Variety Rule (anti-repetition)

To avoid the #1 competitor weakness (samey pages), every consecutive run of pages
must rotate composition type. We cycle through:

- **Establishing scene** (wide view of a space)
- **Vignette** (a small corner / object cluster)
- **Single hero object** (one beautiful subject, lots of space)
- **Pattern/border scene** (decorative framing — used sparingly, never as filler)
- **Cross-section / cutaway** (the cottage opened up — reserved for finale)
- **Character-as-scenery** (a familiar nestled among objects, never a portrait)

No composition type repeats more than twice in a row. Enforced in
`06_Page_Structure.csv`.

## Visual Style Bible (consistency across all 50 pages — and all future volumes)

- **Medium:** clean black-and-white line art, coloring-page style.
- **Line weight:** confident, even outlines; slightly heavier outer contours,
  lighter interior detail. No grayscale, no shading fills, no cross-hatching mass.
- **Background:** pure white. No frames, no borders (unless a page is explicitly a
  border/pattern page).
- **Density:** "balanced detail" — every page has both intricate areas *and* large
  open coloring spaces. Never a wall of tiny lines; never empty.
- **Black areas:** minimal. Solid black is the enemy of a coloring page. Windows,
  cats, and night skies are rendered as *outlines and stars*, not black fills.
- **Recurring motifs (series glue):** a fat cat familiar, a crescent moon, a
  steaming teacup, pumpkins, candles, potion bottles, climbing vines. At least
  two recurring motifs appear in most pages so the world feels continuous across
  volumes.
- **Mood:** cozy, warm, whimsical — Ghibli-meets-cottagecore. Never dark or scary.

## Format Spec

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Trim ratio | 8.5 × 11 in (US Letter, portrait) |
| Resolution | 300 DPI, print-ready |
| Color | Pure black & white line art |
| Pages | 50 unique coloring pages (+ cover, title, how-to, index in PDF) |
| File | High-resolution print-ready PDF |
| Single-sided | Yes (so colorists can remove/frame pages; prevents bleed-through) |

## Front/Back Matter (inside the PDF, beyond the 50 pages)

1. **Cover page** (see `10_Etsy_Listing.md` for cover concept)
2. **Title page** with series branding ("The Cozy Witch's Cottage Series · Vol. 1")
3. **A short welcome note** ("Pour a cup of tea, and step inside…")
4. **How-to / tips page** (paper, markers vs. pencils, print-at-home tips)
5. **The 50 coloring pages**
6. **Page index** (titles + page numbers — see `06_Page_Structure.csv`)
7. **Thank-you + cross-sell page** ("Loved this? The Witch's Library is next…")

## Why This Book Sells

- **Perceived value:** 50 pages + journey + finale = "a real book," not a file.
- **Pinnability:** the finale pages are made to be screenshotted and shared.
- **Series hook:** it openly promises Volume 2, training repeat buyers.
- **Giftability:** cozy, charming, universally safe — an easy gift.
