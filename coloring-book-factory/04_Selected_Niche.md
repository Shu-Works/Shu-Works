## Why This Niche

This isn't a theme — it's a **world**. A witch's cottage is a single, coherent
place made of *rooms*, and each room is a future book:

- The **library** (towers of spellbooks, ladders, candles, a sleeping cat)
- The **apothecary** (potion shelves, dried herbs, bottles, a cauldron)
- The **kitchen** (hanging herbs, copper pots, bread, a window over the garden)
- The **garden & greenhouse** (pumpkins, sunflowers, climbing vines, a well)
- The **familiars** (cats, owls, ravens, toads, hedgehogs napping among objects)
- The **seasons** (the same cottage in spring, autumn, winter, midsummer)

That structure is why it wins as a *factory* niche: Volume 2 doesn't require a
new idea — it requires walking into the next room.

## The Audience Fit

Maps directly onto our primary audience:

- **Adult coloring enthusiasts, women 25–65** — core cottagecore/cozy demographic.
- **Fantasy lovers + cottagecore audience** — this is their exact aesthetic.
- **Stress-relief users** — a cozy, candle-lit world is calming by design.
- **Gift buyers** — "a witch's cottage to color" is an easy, charming gift.
- **Pinterest users** — every page is a pin.

---

## The Tone Guardrail (critical)

This is **cozy witch, not dark witch.** No gore, no pentagrams-as-edginess, no
horror, no occult-as-shock. Think: warm candlelight, a fat cat asleep on a
spellbook, steam rising from a teacup, pumpkins on the windowsill. The feeling is
*Studio Ghibli meets cottagecore*, not *horror*. This keeps it gift-friendly,
Pinterest-safe, and broadly appealing.
