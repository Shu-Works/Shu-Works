# 05 — Book Concept (Book #2)

## Book #2: *The Witch's Library*

> **A Cozy Coloring Journey Through a Witch's Magical Library**
> 50 hand-designed pages · Adult coloring · Printable · 8.5 × 11
> *The Cozy Witch's Cottage Series · Volume 2*

---

## The Premise

Volume 1 opened the cottage door. Volume 2 walks into its most beloved room: the
**library**. The buyer is handed the library key (page 1) and invited to wander
deeper — from a single spellbook to the grand moonlit hall and a library built
into a colossal tree. Same world, same cozy soul, a brand-new room to color.

## Why This Is Volume 2 (the factory thesis, proven)

This book exists to prove the catalog scales by *walking into the next room*, not
inventing a new idea. It reuses everything that made Vol. 1 work:

- **Same recurring motifs** (the cat, owl, raven, crescent moon, steaming teacup)
  so the two books feel like one continuous world and pull buyers into the series.
- **Same journey structure** (Introduction → Exploration → Discovery → Masterpiece)
  and the same progressive difficulty curve.
- **Same tone:** cozy, warm, whimsical — *Ghibli-meets-cottagecore*, never dark.
- **Same production tools** — this folder is a copy of `book01/` with only
  `book_config.py` and the local `06`/`07` CSVs changed.
- **Crossover hooks** to future volumes: a greenhouse-library page (31) and tea
  pages (7, 24) seed the Greenhouse and Tea House volumes.

## The Journey (four chapters)

- **Ch 1 · Introduction (1–12):** single subjects — the key, a spellbook, a lamp,
  a sleeping cat, a globe. Welcoming, open, confidence-building.
- **Ch 2 · Exploration (13–26):** corners & vignettes — the card catalog, reading
  nook, stained-glass window, map table, the archive door.
- **Ch 3 · Discovery (27–40):** full scenes — towering stacks, the spiral book
  tower, the scriptorium, the map room, the librarian's desk.
- **Ch 4 · Masterpiece (41–50):** the showpieces — the grand library at night, the
  infinite stacks, the library cross-section, the library in a tree, and the
  finale uniting every motif.

## Style Bible

Identical to Volume 1 (see `../book01` and `../05_Book_Concept.md`): clean
black-and-white line art, pure white background, balanced detail with large open
coloring spaces, minimal solid black (night skies and animals are *outlines*),
no text, no frames. Line weight and rendering must match Vol. 1 so the series
reads as one hand.

## Format

8.5 × 11 in (US Letter, portrait), 300 DPI, single-sided, print-ready PDF, plus
the standard front/back matter (cover, title, welcome, how-to, index,
thank-you/cross-sell) generated by `package.py`.

The full 50-page plan is in `06_Page_Structure.csv`; prompts in
`07_Generation_Prompts.csv`. Methodology files (`02`, `03`, `08`, `09`, `12`) are
shared from the factory root.
