## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this a physical book?**
No — it's a digital PDF you download instantly and print yourself.

**What size are the pages?**
Standard 8.5 × 11 inches (US Letter), 300 DPI, print-ready.

**Can I print it more than once?**
Yes. Print as many copies as you like for your personal use.

**Single or double-sided?**
Pages are single-sided so you can remove/frame favorites and avoid bleed-through.

**What should I color with?**
Colored pencils, gel pens, and fine-liners work beautifully. Markers are fine on
heavier paper — single-sided pages help prevent bleed-through.

**Do I need special software?**
No — any free PDF reader opens it. Print at home or at a copy/print shop.

**Can I sell my finished/colored pages or the file?**
You may share/sell your own *finished colored artwork*. You may **not** resell,
redistribute, or share the PDF or the line art itself. (See commercial-use notes.)

**Is the content scary?**
No — this is *cozy* witch, not horror. Warm, whimsical, gift-friendly.
