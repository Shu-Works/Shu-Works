## 6. FAQ (homepage / dedicated page)

**Q. How does the Digital PDF work?**
A. Instantly after checkout you'll receive a download link. Files come in
US Letter and A4. Print at home as many times as you like — forever.

**Q. Why choose the Spiral-Bound edition?**
A. It lies 100% flat and folds back on itself — no fighting the spine, no
lost art in the middle margin. If you color with markers or for long
sessions, spiral is the upgrade you'll feel immediately.

**Q. Will markers bleed through?**
A. Pages are printed on one side only, so even if a marker bleeds, it
never ruins another illustration. Slip a scrap sheet behind the page for
heavy alcohol markers. *(Confirm single-sided printing before publishing.)*
